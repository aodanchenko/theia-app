# App look: decided 14 Nov from proto/index.html

- **Earth (veterans, phone):** "On the body", phone version.
  - Full-screen camera with a subtle skeleton.
  - One cue card at the top: "Push your right knee out" plus a short "Like the arrow shows".
  - An arrow at the joint showing which way to move.
  - A huge rep count "7 of 10" bottom-left and a round Stop button bottom-right.
  - Atkinson Hyperlegible, warm paper colours.
- **Space (astronauts, tablet):** "On the body" as the THEIA Workbench, landscape tablet.
  - Menu and toolbar (baseline, model: rehab priors, compare: base model, known cause).
  - Large camera with joint tags and a depth ruler.
  - An Excel-style parameter sheet with a formula bar.
  - A valgus-per-rep chart showing the rehab-model and base-model thresholds.
  - A grid of simulated vitals with sparklines, a status bar and sheet tabs.
  - NASA palette: red #fc3b30, blue #0062b3, greys #a8a8a9 and #b0b1b3, black #000815.
- **Rejected:**
  - "Procedure": text-heavy bottom.
  - "Level": the instrument is clever, but it takes the bottom third away from the camera.
  - "Ledger" Earth as a printout: not accessible enough. Its vintage Space terminal was replaced by the workbench.
  - All earlier rounds: flat silhouette, small camera, generic cards.
