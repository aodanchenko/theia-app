# AGENTS.md: Theia app

Instructions for coding agents (Claude, Codex, Cursor) and humans working in this repo. Read this before changing UI.

## What this is

NASA Space Apps 2026 (Vienna), challenge "Health Monitoring Software for Astronauts". One web app, two modes:

| Mode | Page | Device | User | Job |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Space | `/space` | Tablet, landscape | Astronaut on ARED (squat); walking (Exercise menu, experimental) | Show movement vs personal baseline and the decision: continue / change load / change technique |
| Earth | `/earth` | Phone, portrait | Rehab patient at home | About you (first open) → place the phone (optional) → camera home (Squat / Walk switch): one instruction at a time, what you're doing, rep or second count, start/stop; settings from the gear |

Runs fully in the browser. No backend.

### Rules

- **Colour comes from tokens.** No hex in components. The exception is `SimulatedCamera`'s scene palette (wall, floor, body), which is an illustration.
- **Five colours (Space):** panel greys, ink black, phosphor green (`--good`), amber (`--caution`), red (`--alert`). Blue (`--accent`) only marks our method in charts. Sparklines and focus rings are ink.
- **Labels are engraved placards (Space):** panel titles, table headers, vital names, button labels and the app name are sans, 600, uppercase, letter-spacing .08–.2em, `text-shadow: var(--engraved)`, on a grey panel (the cut only shows on grey, not white). Values and data stay mono, never engraved.
- **Red means "flagged".** `--alert` is only for values past a threshold, and always comes with text or `▲`, never colour alone. One exception: the red **Enable camera** button (`--nasa-red`).
- **Numbers are monospace and tabular** (`--font-mono`, `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums`), right-aligned in tables.
- **Space is dense, with subtle bevels:** 1px `--rule` borders, 3px radius at most, 10–12px type. Depth only via the bevel tokens (raised controls, sunken readouts, grey caps title strips), never drop shadows or gradients. The one exception is the Recommendation panel (20 / 44px).
- **Home is customer-facing:** no hackathon or event references; plain landing (wordmark, headline, two ink-outline buttons); every row a ≥ 44px tap target; safe-area padding.
- **Space needs a wide screen:** phones upright get `RotateGate`; phones sideways get a narrower camera column.
- **Earth is large and calm:** one instruction, one primary button, 18px minimum, touch targets ≥ 44px (Stop is 84px).
- **Motion:** entrances only, once. Use the `enter` class (rise, optional `--delay` in ms) or `pop` (SVG overlays). `prefers-reduced-motion` turns them off. No `transition: all`.
- **Anything simulated says so**, with the visible label `SIMULATED` (see Vitals).

### Design decisions (14 Nov, from the prototype round)

- **Earth camera home = "On you"** (picked from 5 layouts, 14 Nov; tuned in `/lab/earth-style`): the camera framed inside the page; the tip marked on the body at the real joint ("Knee out →", "Chest up ↑", ✓ on target); a plain note under it; one chunky button. A set is 10 reps, then "Set complete · n of 10 on target".
- **Earth look:** Duolingo's *vibe* (flat solid shapes, a solid lip under pressable things, segmented progress, uppercase button labels), not its colours or cartoons. Adult instructor tone for veterans: "Rep 6 · On target", never "Nice!", no XP, flames, medals or mascots. No red, no flashing, no military styling.
- **Space sounds = the same Theia chimes** on live reps (baseline at rep 3, on target, adjust, every 5 on target in a row), with a Sounds tick in the View menu.
- **Earth sounds = Theia chimes** (`capture/chimes.ts`): our own synthesized marimba-like tones, 'juicy' style (mallet click, warm body, bell sparkle, short room). On target, adjust, 5 on target in a row, set complete. Quiet (50%), before the spoken tip, own on/off switch in Settings.
- **No restricted third-party media.** Space Apps terms: non-NASA material must be free *without restriction* or OSI-licensed, and every source cited. So no CC-BY (e.g. Google Material sounds were rejected for this), no CC-BY-SA; CC0, public domain, NASA material or our own work only.
- **Two installable apps (PWA):** Earth and Space install separately (`public/earth.webmanifest`, `public/space.webmanifest`; scope `/earth`, `/space`). Each page is a lazy chunk, and `scripts/pwa.ts` builds `sw-earth.js` / `sw-space.js` that precache only their own page's files, so one app never downloads the other. The pose model, fonts and photos are cached the first time they load. `main.tsx` swaps the manifest, theme colour and icon per route (Earth = Linen & Pine icon, `public/icon-earth*`), registers the worker in production only and asks for persistent storage. Earth keeps the screen awake during a set (`capture/useWakeLock.ts`) and explains installing in Settings (`capture/install.ts`: Android's own prompt as a button, the Share menu on iPhone).
- **Earth languages:** English and Ukrainian. Every Earth string lives in `src/earthText.ts` (formal «ви», gender-neutral); the engine's text functions take a `lang`. EN/УКР switch on the first About-you screen and in Settings; the first visit follows the phone's language; stored apart from About you. Atkinson has no Cyrillic, so Ukrainian uses IBM Plex Sans (`:lang(uk)` in tokens.css); spoken tips use a `uk-UA` voice.
- **Earth palette:** Linen & Pine (OKLCH, one warm neutral hue), green = on target, amber = adjust, always with ✓ / ! / words. Corner scale 0.5 (frame 10 / card 8 / tag 5), lip 4px, counts in IBM Plex (Atkinson's zero is slashed).
- **Rejected for Earth:** Coach (demonstrator figure too small), Signal (least information), Log (busiest), Captions (passive with voice off); a sun mascot (childish); glass pills over the video; Wheat & Sky / Workshop / Clay / Evening palettes (kept in the lab). Note: green vs amber is the weaker pair for red-green colour blindness; Wheat & Sky (blue vs amber) is the safer swap if that becomes a problem.

- **Space console bookends:** NASA black header (`--nasa-black`, white engraved title, light menu hover) on top, the blue status bar at the bottom, light panels between. No other large blue or black surfaces. (Tried and reverted: a NASA-black camera panel with a tinted photo.)
- **Space** = the "THEIA Workbench" look: slim header, large camera with joint tags and a depth ruler, a large recommendation on top of the side column, a parameter table, valgus-per-rep chart with rehab vs base model thresholds, simulated vitals with sparklines. NASA palette: red #fc3b30, blue #0062b3, black #000815.
- **Earth** = "on the body": full-screen camera with a subtle skeleton, one cue card on top ("Push your right knee out"), an arrow at the joint, a huge rep count bottom-left, a round Stop button bottom-right. Atkinson Hyperlegible, warm paper colours.
- **Cut (14 Nov review):** fake menus (File, Edit, Data, Model), decorative toolbar chips (Live/Pause), formula bar, spreadsheet letters/row numbers, sheet tabs: decoration or dead controls. Brought back working: View (panel toggles) and Help (dialogs) menus, the blue status bar. Baseline / model / known cause moved into the recommendation's `basis` line; the camera cue banner merged into the recommendation.
- **Rejected:** a text-heavy "procedure" bottom sheet; a spirit-level instrument (takes the bottom third from the camera); a 1970s printout look for Earth (not accessible enough); visual reskins of Space (hairline, mission control dark, flight data file, soft lab): the original stays.

## Code rules

- TypeScript (strict) + React 19, Vite, CSS Modules. No CSS framework, no UI library.
- Shared data shapes live in `src/types.ts`; type props inline or with a local `Props` interface. Avoid `any` and `!`.
- Imports use real extensions (`./x.ts`, `./X.tsx`): Node runs the `.ts` tests directly, without a build step.
- Smallest change that works. No abstraction, option or wrapper nobody asked for.
- A new component: reuse tokens; add a token only if a value means something new, and add it to both themes when both need it.
- Comments say *why*, in one line. No commented-out code.
- `engine/` stays free of React and DOM, so it can later run on a backend.
- Never commit raw video or datasets.

## Squat model (MVP)

- Live on `/space`: the first 3 detected reps are the personal baseline; each later rep is z-scored against it with the rehab scale (how much one person's correct reps vary, from UI-PRMD). Flag when the worst z passes `EVAL.personal.threshold`; the worst feature picks the cue.
- **Correct form beats "like your baseline".** `FORM_LIMITS` (squat.ts): knee flexion ≥ 90°, knees ≥ foot width, trunk ≤ 45° (textbook), left–right gap ≤ 7° (UI-PRMD). `IDEAL` (generated) = median of all correct reps (≈105°, 1.26, 34°, 3°). Correct form alone flags ~30% of a new person's correct reps (held out), so red flags stay on the personal baseline (98% caught, 11% false) and correct form is the target in every message plus an amber "Adjust form" nudge when a rep or the baseline misses it.
- The recommendation speaks in body terms ("knees bent to 63°, correct ≥ 90° (ideal 105°) · your normal 111° · 3 reps in a row"), never z-scores, and says what to do with the set: repeat the rep, or after 3 flagged in a row, stop and lower the load.
- Before the first live rep: the photo astronaut's set (`DEMO_REPS` in demo.ts): reps 1–9 SIMULATED (decent baseline, then the stance turns uneven and the knees drift in), rep 10 = the photo measured with MediaPipe heavy (`PHOTO_REP`). It runs through the same `liveView` as live reps, so recommendation, table, chart, tags and status bar all describe the man in the picture.
- Reps are detected relative to the person's own standing knee angle (MediaPipe reads straight legs as ~25–30°). Features are averaged over the bottom of the rep. Live scoring widens the rehab scale by `MEDIAPIPE_NOISE` (measured on identical frames), otherwise jitter alone gets flagged.
- Result on UI-PRMD (10 people, leave-one-person-out, 10% false alerts): textbook 83%, rehab population 66%, personal baseline 98%, personal + rehab 98% of bad reps caught. The personal baseline does most of the work; rehab data mainly sets a usable untuned threshold.
- **Motivation, honestly:** a good rep (correct form, within baseline) counts toward the weekly dose (`WEEK` in demo.ts, SIMULATED prescription and earlier sessions; status bar `WEEK n/180`, and the good-rep panel). 3+ good reps show a streak; the This week panel (`WeekDose`) shows the dose and one population fact (Lang 2004: Mir hips lost 1.2–1.5% bone a month; Smith 2012: ARED crews kept theirs). It replaced the model-check panel on the rack screen. Never a personal bone or muscle prediction.
- Left–right asymmetry needs a front-on camera: from a three-quarter view one camera misjudges the far leg (the demo photo read a 22° gap on even legs), so the demo keeps that photo's gap at baseline.
- Limits: 10 healthy people with acted errors, Vicon markers (not MediaPipe), so live accuracy on a phone camera is untested.

## Walking model (experimental)

- Source: the Theia MediaPipe standalone package (Python, offline on a video). `scripts/export-gait.py` exports its frozen models; `engine/gait.ts` ports the pipeline to the browser: MediaPipe 33 → H36M-style 17 joints, 1 s windows of 30 samples every 0.5 s, then (a) the **regional density model** (Gaussian mixture per region and view, fold 0: 4 angle features per region, ranked against reference walks; ≥ P95 = deviation) and (b) the **TCN** (pose-derived criteria such as knee range low / asymmetry high, with the package's evaluability and coverage gates). `gait.test.ts` checks the TS TCN against the package's own PyTorch replay fixture (within 2e-4).
- **Not validated on MediaPipe:** both models were developed on Sapiens2 poses; the package says its scores do not carry over. Everything walking says is labelled experimental. The density model compares with a **reference population**, not the person's own baseline (unlike squats), so its flags are amber "check", never red.
- **Whole body or nothing:** a window is judged only when shoulders, hips, knees and ankles are reliable in 80% of its frames (`wholeBody` in gait.ts); otherwise Earth says "Step into the frame" and Space "Show the whole body". Each region needs only its own joints, so without this gate the hips alone flagged hip hiking with the legs out of view.
- Camera view is guessed per window from shoulder width vs torso length (side / front-rear / unknown = unavailable); the package took it from the user.
- **Named compensations, one tip each** (`compensationFor` in gait.ts, text in `earthText.ts` `comp`): hips tilting (front view) = hip hiking → "Bend your knee as you step"; trunk/shoulders tilting (front) = leaning to the side → "Keep your shoulders level"; trunk leaning (side) = leaning forward → "Stand tall, look ahead"; little knee bend (side) = stiff knee → "Bend your knee more as you step"; the TCN's knee asymmetry = uneven steps → "Make both steps the same length"; little arm swing = stiff arm → "Let your arms swing". Never flagged: the prosthetic knee (About-you side; with a prosthesis but no side, no knee flags), arms of walking-aid users. Not measured: leg swing-out (circumduction). Tips are generic gait cues pending a physiotherapist's review.
- Earth: Squat / Walk switch; a walking set = 10 scored seconds; the note's title is the tip, its line the named compensation and why; a mark on the joint; the report lists each tip with how often. Space: Exercise menu → Walk: the compensation is the decision, the tip the action, the measure the reason; region table (now, typical P05–P95, rank, compensation name), model criteria table; the team will test it on the treadmill case, which the models were not built for (walking on the ground only).
- Training data: LJMU (Topham 2023) and ProGait (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0). Check the Space Apps media terms before submitting with these weights.

## Product rules (from the team's decisions)

- Triage and guidance, never prescription. Every flag carries a cited, pre-approved action. No drug dosing, no disease probabilities.
- **Earth squats and all walking: the common normal, for now** (decided 15 Nov). Every rep is judged on its own against the textbook squat (`BASE_MODEL`, threshold `EVAL.base.threshold` = 10% false alerts on UI-PRMD, 83% of bad reps caught), so a compensation the person already has is caught from rep 1; a personal baseline would absorb it. Depth is not judged with a prosthesis or seated / lying. Walking compares with the reference walks.
- **Space squats: own baseline** (on ARED the feet are anchored and the load is a vacuum cylinder), plus the amber correct-form check.
- Known causes that explain a change: an exercise session, a spacewalk (EVA), early mission (week 1–2).
- Out of scope: pre-flight, ground monitoring, post-flight recovery.
- Offline-first; the core loop needs no cloud.
